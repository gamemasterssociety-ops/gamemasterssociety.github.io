2026 Player Survey

What local players are looking for.

Twenty-six respondents told us how they prefer to play. Their answers help GMS-affiliated Game Masters plan schedules, formats, and future systems around real community interest.

Joining

How It Works

Getting started is simple. Choose a game, visit its Discord channel, and reach out to the Game Master for details on seats, questions, and ticketing.

01

Choose an adventure

Browse the current lineup and find the story, system, format, and schedule that fits the kind of tabletop experience you want.

02

Visit the Discord channel

Each game listing links directly to its Discord channel so you can learn more, ask questions, and see where that table is being organized.

03

Contact the GM

Ask questions, check availability, and arrange your seat directly with the Game Master running that adventure.
