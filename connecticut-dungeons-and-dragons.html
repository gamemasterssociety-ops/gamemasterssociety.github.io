What D&D feels like

Collaborative fantasy with choices, characters, and dice.

In Dungeons & Dragons, each player controls a character while the Game Master presents the world, portrays its people and creatures, and responds to the group's decisions. Players explore, talk, investigate, solve problems, and face dangerous encounters together.

D&D 5e provides a familiar mix of roleplaying, exploration, and tactical combat. The exact balance depends on the adventure and Game Master, so one campaign may feel like heroic fantasy while another leans into mystery, comedy, school drama, or fey intrigue.

New to Dungeons & Dragons?

You do not need to memorize the rules or own every book. Look for listings marked Beginner friendly or All welcome, then read the individual page for anything the Game Master wants you to bring or prepare.

Common questions

Finding a Connecticut D&D game

Do I need Dungeons & Dragons experience to join?

No. Look for games marked Beginner friendly or All welcome. The individual listing explains the expected experience level and how to contact the Game Master.

Are Dungeons & Dragons seats open right now?

Availability changes by campaign. Games marked Recruiting or showing open seats are accepting inquiries. Running games are listed for reference but may not currently have space.

How much do local Dungeons & Dragons games cost?

Prices vary by Game Master and campaign. Each listing shows its per-session price, schedule, format, and sign-up instructions.

Is Game Master Society affiliated with Wizards of the Coast?

No. Game Master Society is an independent tabletop roleplaying organization. Dungeons & Dragons and related trademarks belong to their respective owners.
