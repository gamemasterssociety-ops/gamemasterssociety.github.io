An experience for your people

Your group, your kind of story.

A tabletop RPG is a shared story where players make choices and solve problems together, guided by a Game Master. You do not need to arrive knowing the rules.

How it works

Clear plans, clear responsibilities.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your group

    Share your audience, approximate size, location, goals, and possible dates.

  2. 02

    Plan a format

    We discuss the right game, accessibility needs, venue details, and potential GM availability.

  3. 03

    Agree on the details

    Any coordination fee, GM fee, payment terms, and cancellation expectations are explained in writing before confirmation.

  4. 04

    Enjoy the adventure

    The Game Master runs the agreed session, and GMS helps with the coordination covered by the arrangement.

How pricing and payment work

GMS may charge a separate event-coordination fee. The independent Game Master generally agrees on and receives their own session fee directly from the client unless a specific written event arrangement says otherwise. Pricing depends on the group, format, location, preparation, and availability. We will not ask you to commit before the details are agreed.

Looking to host regular public games at a venue instead? Explore the venue RPG-night pilot. If you publish RPG materials you'd like GMs to explore, use our creator inquiry.
