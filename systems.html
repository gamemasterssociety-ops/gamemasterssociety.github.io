A few survey answers need separate treatment

Homebrew can mean a custom setting, modified rules, or an entirely original system, so it is better described within the individual game listing. Warhammer: The Old World is a miniatures wargame rather than a tabletop roleplaying game, so it is not included in this RPG catalog. Interest in it can still help shape future community events.

The adventure and Game Master matter too

Two games using the same system can still feel completely different. Always read the individual listing for its specific tone, themes, schedule, content notes, experience level, and sign-up process.
