Twilight: 1984 - Survival in Summerville

Small town life collapses after invasion in a Cold War survival story of sacrifice, trust, and resistance.

Visit Game ChannelAll Games
runninglong adventure

The year is 1984 and the Cold War has turned hot. Suddenly, small town life is turned upside down in the aftermath of invasion.

This is a story of survival and sacrifice in a narrative storytelling game with a sprinkle of base building and small unit tactics, inspired by Red Dawn, Jericho, and Alas, Babylon.

You will play as one of Summerville’s townsfolk. As the world collapses around you, survival will depend on more than stockpiled cans and loaded shotguns. You will need to rally neighbors, forge alliances, and decide who can be trusted.

Your actions will determine the fate of Summerville. Good luck, you are on your own.
