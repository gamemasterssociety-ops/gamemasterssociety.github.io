Venue & Community Partnerships

Bring a well-organized RPG night to your space.

Game Master Society helps Connecticut venues offer welcoming tabletop roleplaying events without having to build a program from scratch.

What GMS can provide

A practical event package

01

Game Masters

Connections to GMS-affiliated Game Masters suited to the audience, system, tone, and event format.

02

Player-facing information

Clear listings, beginner guidance, expectations, and promotional support through GMS channels.

03

Event structure

One-shots, beginner nights, recurring tables, themed events, youth programs, or other formats shaped around the venue.

Good fits for partnership

Libraries, cafés, breweries, theaters, game stores, community centers, youth organizations, and other RPG-friendly spaces can all support successful tabletop events.

What we discuss before an event

  • Audience and age range
  • Expected attendance and table capacity
  • Schedule and session length
  • Furniture, noise, accessibility, food, and beverage considerations
  • Promotion and registration responsibilities
  • Pricing or venue costs, if applicable

Independent Game Masters

GMS is a platform, community, and organizing body. GMS-affiliated Game Masters generally operate independently. Event responsibilities, ticketing, and payment arrangements are agreed upon for each opportunity.
