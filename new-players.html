New Player Gateway

Your first tabletop game starts here.

You do not need experience, books, dice, or a prepared character to join a beginner-friendly GMS table unless a specific listing says otherwise.

How to join a game

01

Check the current listings

Look for recruiting games, open seats, one-shots, campaigns, and tables marked beginner-friendly.

02

Read the listing

Review the system, tone, session length, number of sessions, content notes, intended audience, schedule, and experience level.

03

Follow its sign-up instructions

A listing may ask you to visit its Discord channel, message the GM, complete a form, or claim a seat through another posted process.

04

Introduce yourself

For some campaigns, the GM may request a short introduction about your experience, play style, and interest in the game before confirming your seat.

05

Review the expectations

Read the community standards and any table-specific expectations before play begins.

Choosing the right first game

Think about whether you want roleplay, combat, horror, mystery, exploration, or tactical play. Consider whether you prefer a one-shot, a short adventure, or a longer campaign. There is no wrong way to begin. The best first game is one that makes you excited to sit down and play.

What should I bring?

For beginner-friendly games, you generally only need to bring yourself and arrive ready to learn and collaborate. Check the individual listing in case the GM asks players to bring anything specific.

Need help?

We can point you in the right direction.

If you are unsure which system or table fits you, join the Discord or email GMS.
