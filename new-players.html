Your first tabletop game starts here.

You do not need experience, books, dice, or a prepared character to join a beginner-friendly GMS table unless a specific listing says otherwise.

How to join a game

01

Check the open listings

Look for recruiting games, upcoming launches, open seats, one-shots, campaigns, and tables marked beginner-friendly.

02

Read the listing

Review the system, tone, location, price, session length, schedule, content notes, and experience level.

03

Follow the sign-up instructions

Most listings direct you to a Discord channel where you can ask questions and contact the independent Game Master.

04

Review expectations

Read the community standards and any table-specific expectations before play begins.

Choosing the right first game

Think about whether you want roleplay, combat, horror, mystery, exploration, comedy, or tactical play. Consider whether you prefer a one-shot, a short adventure, or a longer campaign. The best first game is one that makes you excited to sit down and participate.

What should I bring?

For beginner-friendly games, you generally only need to bring yourself and arrive ready to learn and collaborate. Check the individual listing in case the GM asks players to bring anything specific.

How tickets usually work

Tickets are usually handled directly by the Game Master running the session unless the event listing says otherwise. Visit the game’s Discord channel for pricing, payment, refund expectations, and other details.

Need help?

We can point you in the right direction.

If you are unsure which table fits, use the private email-preparation form on the homepage or ask in Discord.
