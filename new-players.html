New Player Gateway

New to tabletop games? Start here.

You can try your first tabletop RPG without knowing the rules, owning books or dice, or creating a character ahead of time unless a specific beginner-friendly listing says otherwise. Coming by yourself is normal, too.

Find Beginner-Friendly GamesJoin the GMS Discord

Your first step can be as simple as asking a question. You do not have to commit to a campaign.

How to join your first game

01

Find a beginner-friendly table

Browse games marked Beginner friendly. Check whether seats are currently available, and review the schedule, location, price, tone, and session length. A one-shot is a good way to try a single session.

02

Join the GMS Discord

Join the community server if you are not already a member. You can ask questions before choosing a table.

03

Visit the game’s channel

Return to the game listing and select its Discord link. Ask the independent Game Master to confirm an available seat, the next date, what to bring, how tickets work, and any content or table expectations.

04

Get ready for game night

Check the venue and arrival details and read the community standards. At beginner-friendly tables, rules are explained as you play. The GM can tell you whether characters or materials are provided.

05

Find your next adventure

After your first session, ask in Discord about another one-shot, a short starter campaign, or a continuing table. You do not have to find the next game on your own.

Choosing the right first game

Would you enjoy fantasy, horror, mystery, exploration, or comedy? A one-shot is a standalone session; a campaign continues across multiple sessions. If you are unsure, tell us what sounds fun in Discord. You do not need to be good at acting or memorize game rules.

What should I bring?

For beginner-friendly games, no experience, books, dice, or prepared character are required unless the individual listing says otherwise. Ask the GM about any specific supplies, accessibility needs, and arrival instructions before the session.

How tickets usually work

Tickets are usually handled directly by the Game Master running the session unless the event listing says otherwise. Joining Discord does not automatically reserve a seat. Visit the game’s channel to confirm price, availability, payment, refunds, and player communication with the GM.

It's okay to arrive on your own

Many people come to public games without knowing anyone at the table. You can ask about the group's experience level, the story's tone, and what the session will be like before committing. You are welcome to learn at your own pace.

Need help?

We can point you in the right direction.

If a beginner-friendly table does not currently have a confirmed opening, ask in Discord or send an interest email. We can point you toward future opportunities without promising a seat.
