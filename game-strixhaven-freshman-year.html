Strixhaven: Freshman Year

Curriculum of Chaos

A beginner-friendly magic school adventure where students uncover mysteries beneath Strixhaven University.

runninglong adventure

Dear Prospective Student,

It is our pleasure to announce your formal acceptance to the multiverse's premier magical university, Strixhaven. Here, you will join one of five magical colleges in your pursuit of knowledge and power: Lorehold, Prismari, Quandrix, Witherbloom, or Silverquill.

Whether you seek to unravel the mysteries of The Weave, wield unimaginable cosmic power, or simply get better at talking to ghosts, we can assure you that your time here will be nothing short of inspirational. Please note that Strixhaven University accepts no liability for student death or dismemberment due to eldritch explosions, mauling by magically augmented beasts, or abduction by sinister secret societies.

Once more, congratulations on your acceptance. We look forward to your arrival. Strixhaven, Arcavios awaits.

This adventure covers freshman year of a modified version of Curriculum of Chaos. Players begin to navigate academic life while uncovering the larger mystery beneath the surface of their magical studies at Strixhaven.

Strixhaven offers a fun magic-school D&D experience that is beginner friendly while still offering plenty for seasoned players to enjoy.
