Damn The Man

A silly, heartwarming 90s-inspired adventure about saving your record store before midnight.

Visit Game ChannelAll Games
runningshort adventure

Damn The Man is inspired by movies like Empire Records, Dazed and Confused, and Can't Hardly Wait. It is silly, heartwarming, and packed with 90s energy. No experience is needed to enjoy the game.

Set in 1995, you will juggle the responsibilities of a day that will determine the future of your record store, all while trying to accomplish your big personal goal.

Can you confess your love to your crush and find the money that went missing from the cash box? Can you pay off your debt to your parents while promoting tonight's big show?

The clock is ticking and you have got until midnight to try.
