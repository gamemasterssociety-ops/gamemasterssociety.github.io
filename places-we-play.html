Before you go

Check the game listing.

Schedules, rooms, access instructions, seat availability, and payment details belong to the individual game. Confirm them before traveling.

At the venue

Follow posted rules.

Respect venue staff, shared spaces, other guests, parking guidance, and any table-specific expectations from the Game Master.

Need help?

Ask before the session.

Contact the Game Master through the listing or email GMS if you need help identifying the correct location or an appropriate table.
