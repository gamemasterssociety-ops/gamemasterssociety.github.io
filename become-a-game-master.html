GMS-affiliated Game Masters run in-person games at independently operated venues and rented community spaces.

What we look for

Strong Game Masters build strong tables.

GMS-affiliated Game Masters should be ready to:

  • Run welcoming, respectful tables.
  • Communicate clearly with players.
  • Show up reliably for scheduled sessions.
  • Explain table expectations before play begins.
  • Support new and experienced players.
  • Use safety and consent tools appropriate to the game.
  • Represent Game Master Society professionally and with a community-minded approach.
  • Work within shared GMS standards when using GMS spaces, resources, promotional channels, or venue opportunities.
A good fit matters

Experience is only part of the conversation.

We also want to understand what you enjoy running, how you communicate, your availability, and your comfort with public games and new players.

Independent Game Masters

Your games remain your responsibility.

GMS-affiliated Game Masters operate independently and manage their own games, pricing, ticketing, taxes, and refund decisions unless a specific event arrangement says otherwise.

How to get started

A conversation before affiliation.

The interest form gives us the basics. If there may be a good fit, we will arrange a short in-person conversation before moving forward.

01

Complete the form

Tell us about your experience, preferred systems, play style, availability, and the games you want to run.

02

Meet with GMS

We will discuss public play, new-player support, table expectations, safety tools, and whether GMS fits your goals.

03

Review shared standards

If the fit feels right, we will go over communication, scheduling, Discord use, listings, venue expectations, and public-facing language.

What affiliation may offer

Resources for better games and stronger connections.

Depending on availability and the type of game or event, GMS-affiliated Game Masters may have access to:

Community

A local network

GM-only Discord spaces, feedback, collaboration, and connections with local players and other Game Masters.

Support

Tools and development

Shared resources, GM training, workshops, and templates for listings, player communication, and table expectations.

Opportunities

Public games and venues

Promotion for public games and possible venue opportunities when they fit the Game Master, audience, and location.
