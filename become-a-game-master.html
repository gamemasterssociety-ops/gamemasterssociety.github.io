What we look for

Reliable Game Masters build reliable tables.

  • Welcoming and respectful public play
  • Clear, timely communication with players
  • Dependable attendance and scheduling
  • Comfort explaining expectations before play begins
  • Support for new and experienced players
  • Safety and consent practices appropriate to the game
  • Professional use of GMS spaces, promotional channels, resources, and venue opportunities
A good fit matters

Experience is not the only factor.

We also want to understand what you enjoy running, your availability, your communication style, and whether you are ready to launch a public game.

Frequently asked questions

What affiliation does and does not mean.

Does GMS employ Game Masters?

No. GMS-affiliated Game Masters operate independently.

Who sets prices?

The Game Master normally sets their own ticket price and manages payment, taxes, refunds, and player communication.

Does GMS guarantee players or venues?

No. GMS may provide promotion and possible opportunities, but cannot guarantee attendance, revenue, or venue placement.

Is GMS only for D&D?

No. GMS welcomes many tabletop roleplaying systems, genres, campaign lengths, and play styles.

Can I bring an existing game?

Potentially. Public listings should be clear, reliable, and consistent with GMS community standards.

Does submitting guarantee affiliation?

No. Access to GMS spaces, promotional channels, venue opportunities, and shared resources depends on fit and availability.
