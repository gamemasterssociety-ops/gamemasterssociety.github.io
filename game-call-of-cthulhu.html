Call of Cthulhu

Eat Your Heart Out

A speed-dating night in Holmouth turns into a race against time, horror, and unraveling sanity.

Visit Game ChannelAll Games
runningshort adventure

Our story takes place in Holmouth, New Hampshire. Holmouth is a fictional town on the coast of New England where many secrets lie hidden.

Eat Your Heart Out is a race against time. We begin at Gannets restaurant for a local speed dating event where lonely souls can find someone to give their hearts to. Not all is as it seems at this event. There are those who wish to meddle in things that ought to be left alone.

Can you escape with your heart and mind unbroken?

Call of Cthulhu is centered around investigating cosmic horrors and attempting to maintain sanity in a world of madness. Characters are ordinary people facing secrets humanity was never meant to know. The true horror lies in the unknown, psychological torment, and the realization that people are nothing compared to the things they were never meant to understand.
